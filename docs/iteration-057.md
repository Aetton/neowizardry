# Iteration 057 — player-facing combat entry

The reported blocker was the ordinary player's ability to start the beach crab fight. The previous smoke called the battle-entry method directly and did not exercise dispatched keyboard input. Player hotkeys used logical Keycode, so E/B/I depended on the keyboard layout; rejected entry wrote to hidden research HUD labels. Combat also required a three-unit radius and line of sight to an arbitrary human-height point above a creature, with no visible attack affordance.

Player hotkeys now use physical keys (falling back to logical keys for events without physical identity). B explicitly requests the nearest available encounter; E/click still prioritizes item pickup, then an encounter in front of the view, then doors. The normal HUD displays encounter name/count, distance, B attack availability or a visible reason to approach/go around an obstruction. The Esc menu also has an explicit attack button. Completed encounters remain completed; use New group for a fresh run, which retains a backup of the previous campaign.

Ordinary player attack range is eight world units, a usability policy rather than recovered native aggro behavior. Visibility is checked against the original idle-body bounds (center/top with a small surface tolerance), considering all group members rather than rejecting the group solely because its closest member is obscured. Idle bounds remain stable across cosmetic attack/death animation frames. The existing research entry policy remains separate. Starting combat still captures the exploration checkpoint and uses the same persisted encounter, party, inventory, battle and reward state.

Player battle instructions, defeat/reload guidance and the next-round button are in Russian. Round actions cannot execute behind a paused game menu. Source assets, original placements and campaign schemas/hashes are unchanged; no content re-preparation or new campaign is required for the runtime change.

Validation: client build with zero warnings/errors, existing 4227 core/input checks, local ordinary-start and encounter smoke. The new ordinary-start regression dispatches physical B/E events with Cyrillic logical keys, verifies out-of-range feedback, finds an original-collision standable approach, dispatches B and checks that the actual battle panel opens. The prior save/reload, pickup, migration, battle completion and once-only rewards checks remain enabled. This is automated headless interaction testing, not a manual visual walkthrough of the whole monastery.

Packaged Windows and original-data CI results are recorded after completion. TOC-44 remains in progress; this iteration fixes interaction usability rather than adding containers/keys or declaring the floor complete.

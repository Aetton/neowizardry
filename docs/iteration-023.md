# Iteration 023 — connected projectile post-contact resolution

Traced the producer of global projectile status +0x8C4 through actor collision callback `0x4A4720`: ordinary actor contact can be intercepted using a resolved actor byte and a separate d100. A zero interception byte takes no draw. The callback supplies the actual collision target; it can differ from the selected target. Interception-byte derivation and spatial collision remain separate actor/world policies.

Traced the character consumer: projectile contact bypasses the melee hit comparison, then consumes a fresh d100 for penetration. Ordinary shooting/throwing does not inherit melee's natural 1–5 automatic penetration or 96–100 natural misses. Failed penetration consumes no damage dice. The separate trait/hand/weapon automatic-penetration branch is explicitly outside neutral scope, rather than silently enabled for every projectile.

Added `OriginalProjectileStrike` for ordinary post-collision interception and post-delivery penetration/damage. Added `OriginalProjectileAttack.Complete` to compose the calculated result with exactly one selected-source resource completion. Miss, interception and stopped penetration all spend the projectile; penetrating damage uses recovered physical scaling, including possible full absorption. Current-state validation rejects stale/foreign/duplicate completion and invalid draw plans without partial inventory changes. Caller-owned HP application, target identity, prepared weapon/source facts and RNG remain explicit.

Validation: 955 portable C# checks including gestures (62 added), 962 with the private 819-item catalog, 956 with private spell metadata/power scenarios (826 combinations, not actor eligibility). 27 Python tests pass. Client build has zero warnings/errors. Real-data Godot gameplay and synthetic mobile smokes pass. Exhaustive d100 sweeps at ten penetration thresholds, shooting/throwing boundaries, draw-consumption rules, interception, absorbed damage, one-cost completion, in-flight incapacity, five-swing continuation through source replacement and pending save replay are covered.

This is a connected **post-delivery** path, not full ranged-hit reconstruction or live Godot integration. Aim vector/spread, world collision and actual target selection are external; no synthetic final hit probability is invented. Native interception-byte derivation, special auto-penetration, conditions/location defenses, end-of-attack refill ordering and complete actor/hand policy remain open. The training battle remains labelled fixture. Next: effective actor/defense and delivery integration, followed by one representative original magic damage/healing effect path.
